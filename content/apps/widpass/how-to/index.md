**WidPass - QR & Barcode Widget**

Turn a photo of a code into a pass you can pull up from the home screen.

## Add a code from a photo

Tap **+** and pick a photo or screenshot from your library. WidPass finds the QR code or barcode and shows it in a green box. Only what's inside the box is saved. If it's off, drag the box or pinch to adjust it. Give it a label and save.

New here? Try the sample QR code or barcode first. The guide is under **?** at the top right.

## Fix the area later

Touch and hold a saved pass and tap **Adjust Area** to re-crop it, or use **Auto Detect** to find the code again.

## Use it from the widget

Add the WidPass widget to your home screen. Tap **◀ ▶** to move between your passes without opening the app at all.

## Show it at the counter

Tap the widget to open the code full-screen. Screen brightness goes to maximum automatically, so scanners read it the first time.

## Open a pass with Shortcuts

In the Shortcuts app, add the **Open Pass** action and pick a pass. Running the shortcut opens that pass full-screen, ready to scan.

## Keep the list tidy

Rename labels and change the order whenever you want.

## Good to know

- Everything stays on your device. No cloud sync, no tracking, no account.
- No ads and no in-app purchases - the app is free.
