**WidPass - QR & Barcode Wallet**

All your passes, one tap away.

## Collect your codes in one place

Keep the QR codes and barcodes you use — membership, checkout, entry — together instead of scattered across photos and apps.

## Use a code from the widget

Put the WidPass widget on your home screen and open the code you need from there, without hunting through an app first.

## Good to know

- Made for the moment people are waiting behind you at a counter or a gate.
- iOS app.
