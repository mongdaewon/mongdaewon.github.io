**WidPass - QR & Barcode Wallet**

Turn a photo of a code into a pass you can pull up from the home screen.

## Add a code from a photo

Tap **+** and pick a photo from your library. WidPass finds the QR code or barcode in it and crops it for you. Give it a label and save.

## Use it from the widget

Add the WidPass widget to your home screen. Tap **◀ ▶** to move between your passes without opening the app at all.

## Show it at the counter

Tap the widget to open the code full-screen. Screen brightness goes to maximum automatically, so scanners read it the first time.

## Keep the list tidy

Rename labels and change the order whenever you want.

## Good to know

- Everything stays on your device. No cloud sync, no tracking, no account.
- No ads and no in-app purchases — the app is free.
