Pick several clips, put them in order, and trim or split away what you don't need.  
Add text, music, sound effects, stickers, and photos, or record a voice-over on the timeline.  
Set transitions and clip speed, and choose an original, 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9 frame.  
Save in 1080p with no watermark and no ads.  
Editing happens on your Android phone, and your videos are never uploaded.  
Free: save videos up to 1 minute long and keep 1 project. JoinEdit Plus removes both limits, as a 1-week or 1-year subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.
