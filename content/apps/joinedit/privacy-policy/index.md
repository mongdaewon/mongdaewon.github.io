**JoinEdit - Video Editor**

Last updated: November 19, 2026

*This Privacy Policy is provided in English only.*

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how JoinEdit ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Analytics Data

The app uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve the app experience:

- App usage patterns and feature interactions (for example, which screens are opened, when a project is created or opened, and whether saving a video finished or failed)
- Device type and operating system version
- Plus purchases: which plan was bought and its price, never your payment details

This data is collected automatically and cannot be used to identify you personally. The app does not use an advertising ID.

### 2. Local Data

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

- Your projects: the clips you picked and the edits you make (text, stickers, photos, music, sound effects, voice recordings, transitions)
- Photos, stickers, voice recordings and music files you add to a project, copied into the app's own folder
- App settings and your Plus purchase status

**All video editing and saving happens entirely on your device.** Your videos, photos, recordings and their contents are never uploaded to our servers or any third party.

## Photo Library Access

The app lets you pick videos and photos with the system Photo Picker, so no storage or media permission is needed. To reopen your projects later, the app keeps read access only to the items you picked; deleting a project releases that access. Finished videos are saved to your gallery.

## Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you use the recording tool to add your own voice to a video. Recordings are saved inside the app and used only in your project and the videos you save. They are never uploaded.

## Music Files

If you choose a music file from your device, the app copies only that file into its own folder so it can be used in your project. The app does not scan or browse your files.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Contacts or calendar data
- Your videos, photos, recordings, or their contents (processed only on your device)

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase Analytics** ([Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy))
- **Google Play Billing** (Android) / **Apple StoreKit** (iOS) - For in-app purchases, handled entirely by Google or Apple

## Advertising

JoinEdit does not display any advertisements. We do not use any advertising SDKs.

## In-App Purchases

JoinEdit offers JoinEdit Plus. Free saves videos up to 1 minute long and keeps 1 project; Plus removes both limits.

Plus is a 1-week or 1-year auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. You can cancel a subscription at any time in your Google Play or App Store account settings.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google (Google Play) or Apple (App Store). We never see or store your payment details. The app only receives, and keeps on your device, whether your purchase or subscription is active.

## Data Security

Your local data is stored securely on your device. We do not have access to this data. Analytics data is handled by Google according to their security standards.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

- **Delete local data:** Deleting a project removes its data from the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data and stops all analytics collection
- **Limit tracking:** You can manage app tracking and analytics permissions in your device's privacy settings

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
