**AudioJoin - Merge and Trim Audio Files**

Last updated: September 5, 2026

*This Privacy Policy is provided in English only.*

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how AudioJoin ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

**AudioJoin collects nothing.** The app has no analytics, no accounts, and no server of its own. It does not connect to the internet to do its work.

### Local Data

The following stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

- The audio files you import, and the copies the app makes of them while you edit
- Your clip order and the trim points you set
- The files you export

Imported copies live in a temporary folder and are removed when you remove the clip or the system clears that folder.

## File Access

The app opens audio only when you choose it — through the file picker, or by sharing a file into AudioJoin from another app such as Voice Memos. A file shared into AudioJoin is placed in a private folder that only AudioJoin and its share extension can read.

**All audio processing happens entirely on your device.** Your recordings and their contents are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal information (name, email address, phone number)
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, or any data outside the audio files you pick
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- Advertising identifiers

## Third-Party Services

AudioJoin uses no third-party services. No SDK in the app sends data anywhere.

The app includes the LAME MP3 encoder as an open source library so it can write MP3 files. It is a library, not a service: it runs on your device and sends nothing. See [Open Source](../open-source/) for its license and source code.

## Advertising

AudioJoin does not display any advertisements. We do not use any advertising SDKs.

## In-App Purchases

AudioJoin does not offer any in-app purchases. The app is completely free.

## Data Security

Because your audio never leaves your device, there is no transmission to secure and no copy of it on any server we run. Files are protected by your device's own storage protections.

## Children's Privacy

AudioJoin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your Rights

All data the app holds is on your device and under your control. Deleting a clip removes the app's copy of it, and deleting the app removes everything the app has stored. Your original files stay where they were.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
