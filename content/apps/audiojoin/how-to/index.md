**AudioJoin - Merge and Trim Audio Files**

Pick several recordings, cut what you don't need, and save them as one file.

## Merge recordings into one

Pick the recordings you want and put them in the order you choose. They are joined into a single file.

## Trim the start and end

Each recording shows its waveform, so you can see where the useful part begins. Trim the start and end to a tenth of a second.

## Choose the output format

Save as M4A, MP3, or WAV. MP3 is encoded on your device, so the file opens anywhere.

## Send files in from another app

Voice memos, interviews, and lectures can come straight in from the share sheet, without saving them first.

## Good to know

- Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is uploaded.
