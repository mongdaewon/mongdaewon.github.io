Stack photos in 1, 2, or 3 columns, line them up side by side, or place 2 to 6 in a grid.  
Single-column, side-by-side, and grid layouts don't shrink any photo, unless the result passes the size limit. The app tells you when it does.  
Add captions and a title, or keep just the subtitle strip of each photo after the first.  
Pick a ratio for posting, such as 1:1, 4:5, or 9:16, or set the exact size in pixels.  
No ads and no watermark. Your photos never leave your iPhone or iPad.  
Free: combine up to 3 photos into one image. JoinPhoto Plus, a one-time purchase, removes that limit.
