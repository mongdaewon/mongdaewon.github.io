**JoinPhoto**

Last updated: November 24, 2026

*This Privacy Policy is provided in English only.*

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how JoinPhoto ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Analytics Data

The app uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve the app experience:

- App usage patterns and feature interactions
- Device type and operating system version
- Plus purchases: which plan was bought and its price (on Android, also the Google Play order ID), never your payment details

This data is collected automatically and cannot be used to identify you personally.

### 2. Local Data

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

- Copies of the photos you pick, your layout, captions and edits
- App settings and preferences
- Your Plus purchase status

**All image processing happens entirely on your device.** Your photos and their contents are never uploaded to our servers or any third party.

## Photo Library Access

The app uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose. On iPhone and iPad, it asks for permission only to add the finished image to Photos. Selected photos are read and combined entirely on your device, and are never uploaded anywhere.

On Android, finished images are saved to your gallery, and no storage or media permission is needed.

If you choose images from the Files app (iPhone and iPad) or the system file picker (Android) instead, the app copies only those images into its own folder. It does not scan or browse your files.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Contacts or calendar data
- Your photos or their contents (processed only on your device)

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase Analytics** ([Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy))
- **Apple StoreKit** (iOS) / **Google Play Billing** (Android) - For in-app purchases, handled entirely by Apple or Google

## Advertising

JoinPhoto does not display any advertisements. We do not use any advertising SDKs.

## In-App Purchases

JoinPhoto offers a one-time in-app purchase (JoinPhoto Plus). Free combines up to 3 photos per image; Plus removes that limit. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive or store your payment details.

## Data Security

Your local data is stored securely on your device. We do not have access to this data. Analytics data is handled by Google according to their security standards.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

- **Delete local data:** Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data and stops all analytics collection
- **Limit tracking:** You can manage app tracking and analytics permissions in your device's privacy settings

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
