Hold ⌃⌥ and press an arrow — the window snaps, and pressing again cycles half, two thirds, one third.  
Keep going in the same direction and it carries on to your next display, so multi-monitor work stops meaning drag, drop, resize.  
Nothing to configure and nothing to learn: the shortcuts follow the key position, so they work in any input source.
