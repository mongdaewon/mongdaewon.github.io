Every address below was checked against the live site. Tap **Add** on an iPhone with QuickSearch installed and the app opens with the fields filled in - you decide whether to save.

Then type the keyword in the Safari address bar, a space, and what you are looking for: `yt swiftui`.

Korean sites use hangul initials as keywords (`ㄴ` for Namu Wiki), so a Korean typist never has to switch keyboards mid-search. Any script works the same way - the extension compares text, not language.
