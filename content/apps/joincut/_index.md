Pick just the parts you want and make one clean video.  
Or merge whole videos into one, in the order you choose.  
No re-encoding, so quality stays original and saving is instant.  
Works on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac.  
On a Mac you can open clips straight from Finder and save the result wherever you want.
