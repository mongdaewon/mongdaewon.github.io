**JoinCut - Lossless Video Merge**

Join clips without touching the quality.

## Merge whole videos

Add the videos you want and set the order. They become one video, joined in the order you chose.

## Keep only the parts you want

Instead of a whole clip, pick just the parts worth keeping. What you picked becomes one clean video.

## Save with no quality loss

JoinCut does not re-encode. The quality stays exactly as the original, and saving finishes almost instantly.

## On a Mac

JoinCut runs on Apple Silicon Macs. There you can open clips straight from Finder and save the result wherever you want.

## Good to know

- Works on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac.
- No re-encoding means the saved file keeps the original quality, not a re-compressed copy.
