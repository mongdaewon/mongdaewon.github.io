**Where Is My Cursor - Mouse Highlighter**

Keep your pointer easy to follow in presentations, screen shares, online classes, and recordings.

## Turn on the mouse halo

A bright ring follows your cursor everywhere. Pick a circle, square, or rounded square, then set the color, size, thickness, opacity, and glow to suit the screen you're on. It can also hide itself when the mouse stops moving.

## Dim everything but the cursor

Hold **Control** and the screen darkens except for the area around your pointer. Use it when you want the room looking at one spot and nothing else. Pick a circle, square, or rounded square, and scroll while holding **Control** to change the size of the bright area.

## Draw on the screen

Hold **Option** and move the mouse to draw over whatever is on screen. Press **Command** to erase everything you drew.

- **Option + 1 to 5** switches between freehand, arrow, rectangle, circle, and numbered markers.
- With numbered markers chosen, hold **Option** and click to stamp 1, 2, 3 in order.
- **Option + Z** undoes the last stroke. Markers stay.
- Scroll while holding **Option** to change the line width or marker size.
- Turn on automatic erasing and your drawings fade out after a few seconds.

## Add a sound to every click

Click sounds play on every click. Choose one of six built-in macOS system sounds and set the volume.

## Shortcuts

- **Control + Option + M** turns the app on or off from anywhere.
- Spotlight, drawing, and erase can each require **Shift** as well, so they never go off by accident.

## Good to know

- The app collects nothing. No network connection, no analytics, no tracking, and every setting stays on your Mac.
- Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
