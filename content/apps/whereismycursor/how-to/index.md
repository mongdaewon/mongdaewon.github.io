**Where Is My Cursor - Mouse Highlighter**

Keep your pointer easy to follow in presentations, screen shares, online classes, and recordings.

## Turn on the mouse halo

A bright ring follows your cursor everywhere. Pick a circle, square, or rounded square, then set the color, size, thickness, border style, and glow to suit the screen you're on.

## Dim everything but the cursor

Hold **Control** and the screen darkens except for the area around your pointer. Use it when you want the room looking at one spot and nothing else.

## Draw on the screen

Hold **Option** and move the mouse to draw freely over whatever is on screen. Press **Command** to erase everything you drew.

## Add a sound to every click

Click sounds play on every click, using built-in macOS system sounds.

## Good to know

- The app collects nothing. No network connection, no analytics, no tracking, and every setting stays on your Mac.
- Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
