**Where Is My Cursor - Mouse Highlighter**

Make the pointer impossible to lose during a presentation or a screen share.

## Put a halo around the cursor

A halo follows the pointer so every eye lands on it, even on a large or shared screen.

## Dim everything but the cursor

The spotlight darkens the rest of the screen and leaves the area around the pointer lit. Use it when you want attention on one spot only.

## Draw on the screen

Draw directly over whatever is on screen to mark what you are talking about.

## Good to know

- Built for presentations and screen sharing, where "where is the mouse?" costs you the room.
- macOS app.
