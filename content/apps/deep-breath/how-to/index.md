**Deep Breath - Breathing Exercise**

Breathe along with the screen. That is the whole app.

## Breathe along with the screen

Open the app and match your breathing to what the screen shows. A busy mind settles in a few minutes.

## Make it a daily habit

Do it every day and stress never piles up. A few minutes a day is enough to stay calm and focused.

## Good to know

- No class and no equipment. Just the screen and your breath.
