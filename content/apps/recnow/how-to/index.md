**RecNow - Screen Recorder**

Open it, press the red button, and you are recording.

## Start recording

Open RecNow and press the red button. There is no setup screen to get through first.

## Choose what sound to capture

Record the system audio, your own voice through the microphone, or both together.

## Good to know

- Every recording is free up to 10 minutes.
- Recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded.
- No ads interrupt you, and there is no subscription.
