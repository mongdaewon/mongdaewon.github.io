Every address below was checked against the live site.  
Tap **＋** on an iPhone with Jumpbar installed and the app opens with the fields filled in - you decide whether to save.

Then type the keyword in the Safari address bar, a space, and what you are looking for: `yt swiftui`.

The left column is the keyword for that site.  
**You can change it to anything you like before saving in the app** - the value here is only a suggestion.

Korean sites use hangul initials as keywords (`ㅂ` for Naver Blog), so a Korean typist never has to switch keyboards mid-search. Any script works the same way - the extension compares text, not language.

Tap the address on the right of a name to open that site in a new tab.
