**Jumpbar - Keyword Search Shortcuts**

Type a keyword, then your search. Safari lands on that site's results.

## Before you start

Jumpbar is a Safari extension, so turn it on in Safari's extension settings first.

## Search a site from the address bar

Type the keyword, a space, then what you are looking for. `yt coffee` takes you straight to YouTube results — not to a search page you still have to click through.

## Add your own site

Search that site once in Safari and copy the address of the results page. Paste it into Jumpbar and the app works out the rest.

## Add ready-made sites

Browse the [keyword gallery](../gallery/) and add the sites you use often with one tap.

## Good to know

- Your search words never leave your iPhone.
- No account, no tracking, no ads.
