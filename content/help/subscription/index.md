This applies to all Mongdaewon apps, including JoinSound, JoinCut and AudioJoin.
Payments go through the App Store and Google Play, so you cancel and request refunds directly with Apple or Google. Apple and Google decide on refunds.

## Before you go, did something bother you?

Tell us with **Send Feedback** in the app's help, or at [support@mongdaewon.com](mailto:support@mongdaewon.com). We read every message and fix what we can.

## iPhone and iPad {#iphone}

### Cancel a subscription

1. Open **Settings** and tap your name at the top.
2. Tap **Subscriptions**.
3. Tap the app, then tap **Cancel Subscription**.

If there is no **Cancel Subscription** button, the subscription is already cancelled.

### Request a refund

1. Sign in to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) with your Apple Account.
2. Choose **I'd like to**, then **Request a refund**.
3. Choose the reason, then **Next**.
4. Choose the app or subscription, then **Submit**.

Apple usually updates you within 24 to 48 hours. You can [check the status](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118224) of your request. After Apple approves it, the money can take extra time to reach your payment method.

## Android {#android}

### Cancel a subscription

1. On your phone, open [subscriptions in Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
2. Choose the app.
3. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the instructions.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

### Request a refund

Go to [Google Play refund requests](https://support.google.com/googleplay?p=refundAWF) and choose the order.
Google usually decides within one day, and at most within four days.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Does Plus stop as soon as I cancel?

No. Plus stays on until the end of the period you already paid for. After that, you are not charged again.

### Do I need to cancel a lifetime purchase?

No. A lifetime purchase is a one-time payment, so you are never charged again and there is nothing to cancel. To get a refund, ask Apple or Google the same way as above.
